Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

We want you to love what you ordered. If something is not right, this page explains your options. It is written to be consistent with South Africa's Consumer Protection Act (CPA) and Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA).

  • Before it ships: cancel on WhatsApp for a full refund.
  • 7-day cooling-off: change your mind within 7 days of collecting your parcel and return it for a full refund.
  • Faulty or wrong item: we fix it, replace it, or refund you, and we cover the return cost.
  • Size exchanges: available, subject to stock.

1. Cancelling Before Dispatch

If your order has not been dispatched yet, message us on WhatsApp or email discreteclothing24@gmail.com with your order details and we will cancel it and refund you in full. Once an order has been dispatched, it is treated as a return (see below).

2. 7-Day Cooling-Off Period

If you bought from us online, you may cancel the purchase within 7 days of receiving your goods (the day you collect the parcel from the PEP store or PAXI Point), without giving a reason and without a penalty.

  • Tell us within the 7 days by WhatsApp or email, then send the item back within 7 days of telling us.
  • You are responsible for the direct cost of returning the item to us.
  • You get a full refund of what you paid, including the original delivery fee, within 30 days of cancelling.
  • The item must meet the return conditions in section 5.

The cooling-off right does not apply to goods made to your personal specifications.

3. Faulty, Damaged, or Wrong Items

If your item arrives faulty, damaged, or is not what you ordered, contact us as soon as you can, ideally with photos. Under the CPA you can return defective goods within 6 months of delivery. We will, at your choice and where possible, repair or replace the item, or refund you in full. We pay the return delivery cost for these items.

4. Size Exchanges

Wrong size? We will gladly exchange it for another size, subject to stock. Message us within 7 days of collecting your parcel. Unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong one, you cover the delivery of the return parcel and the delivery of the replacement at our standard PAXI rates (see below). If your size is out of stock, we will offer a refund instead.

5. Conditions for Returns

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition, with any tags attached.
  • Please include your order details so we can match your return to your order.
  • Please look after the item while it is with you. We may reduce a refund if an item comes back worn, washed, or damaged by you.

6. How to Return Something

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or email discreteclothing24@gmail.com with your order details and the reason.
  2. We will reply with the return details and the best way to send it back.
  3. Pack the item securely and send it to us. Keep your parcel receipt or tracking details until your refund or exchange is complete.

7. Delivery Fees

We use PEP PAXI. There is no free shipping. Delivery prices depend on the bag size and speed:

8. How & When You Get Your Refund

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method (paid through PayFast).
  • We refund within 30 days of the cancellation, and usually sooner.
  • Refunds are processed once we have received and checked the returned item (except for orders cancelled before dispatch).

9. Lost, Delayed, or Uncollected Parcels

  • Lost in transit: every PAXI bag includes basic cover up to R500, and extra cover can be added (a R10 voucher covers up to R2,500, a R20 voucher up to R5,000). If your parcel is confirmed lost, contact us and we will log it with PAXI and either resend your order or refund you.
  • Delayed: delivery times are estimates. Contact us if your parcel has not arrived after the estimated time, and we will follow it up.
  • Not collected: if a parcel is not collected within the period stated in your SMS and is returned to us, we will contact you to arrange re-delivery (a new delivery fee applies) or to refund you the price of the items less the delivery costs already paid.

10. Contact

Need help with a cancellation, return, or exchange? Reach us on WhatsApp or at discreteclothing24@gmail.com. See also our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

This page is a plain-language summary of our cancellation and refund process. It does not limit your rights under South African consumer law and is not a substitute for formal legal advice.
