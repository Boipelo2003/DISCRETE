Returns & Exchanges

01

Check Eligibility

Items must be returned within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition with all tags attached. Sale items and accessories are final sale and cannot be returned.

02

Contact the Studio

Email discreteclothing24@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp with your order details and reason for return. We'll respond within 1 business day.

03

Send the Item Back

Once your return is approved, ship the item back to us. Return shipping costs are covered by the customer unless the item is defective or we made an error.

04

Refund or Exchange

Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process your refund or send the replacement size within 3–5 business days. Refunds are issued via EFT to your bank account.

Defective or Incorrect Items — If you receive a defective item or the wrong product, contact us immediately. We will cover all return shipping costs and either replace the item or issue a full refund. Please send photos of the issue via WhatsApp or email.

Size Exchanges — Not sure about your size? Check our Sizing Guide before ordering. For size exchanges, contact us within 7 days of delivery. Subject to stock availability.
