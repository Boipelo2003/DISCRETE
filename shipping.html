Shipping Details

Last updated: November 2026
  • Courier: PEP PAXI (store-to-store), within South Africa.
  • Cost: from R59.95, depending on bag size and speed. We do not currently offer free shipping.
  • Time: packed within 1–2 business days, then 7–9 business days (Standard) or 3–5 business days (Express).
  • Collection: pick up at a PEP store or PAXI Point with your SMS code.

1. How Delivery Works

  1. You order and payPay securely through PayFast, then send us your order confirmation on WhatsApp.
  2. We pack your orderUsually within 1–2 business days of confirmed payment.
  3. We send it with PEP PAXIYour parcel is dropped off for delivery to the PEP store or PAXI Point you chose.
  4. You get an SMSPAXI sends a collection code to your cell phone number when your parcel is ready.
  5. You collect itTake the SMS code and your ID to the store and collect your parcel.

2. Delivery Costs & Times

We choose the bag size for you based on what's in your order, and you choose the speed at checkout. Delivery times are counted in business days from dispatch.

  • Large Bag (64cm × 51cm) fits up to 10 kg. This suits tracksuits and bigger orders.
  • There is no free shipping at the moment. The delivery fee is based on the table above.
  • Very large orders may need more than one bag. The delivery fee is charged per bag, and checkout always shows the exact amount before you pay.
  • Delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed. Delays can happen at the courier's end.
  • We currently deliver within South Africa only.

3. Collecting Your Parcel

  • Give us the correct cell phone number, because your collection code is sent there by SMS.
  • Choose the PEP store or PAXI Point that is easiest for you to reach.
  • Collect your parcel within the period stated in your PAXI SMS.
  • If a parcel isn't collected in time, it is returned to us. We'll contact you to arrange re-delivery (a new delivery fee applies) or a refund of the item price less the delivery costs already paid. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

4. Cover for Lost Parcels

Every PAXI bag includes basic cover of up to R500 for lost parcels. Because some orders (such as tracksuits) cost more than that, extra cover is available through PAXI cover vouchers:

  • R10 voucher covers up to R2,500
  • R20 voucher covers up to R5,000

If you would like extra cover, message us on WhatsApp before your order is dispatched. If a parcel is confirmed lost, contact us and we'll log it with PAXI and either resend your order or refund you.

5. Returns & Exchanges

Not right? Here is the short version. The full rules are in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

  • Cancel before dispatch: full refund. Message us on WhatsApp.
  • 7-day cooling-off: tell us within 7 days of collecting your parcel and send the item back unworn, unwashed, and with tags, for a full refund. You pay the return delivery.
  • Faulty or wrong item: we repair, replace, or refund, and we cover the return delivery.
  • Size exchange: available, subject to stock.

To start a return or exchange, contact us first so we can give you the return details.
