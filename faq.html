Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about sizing, ordering, delivery, and returns.

Sizing

How do I find my size?

Check our Sizing Guide and compare it with a piece of clothing you already own and like the fit of. If you are between sizes, most people size up for a roomier, streetwear fit.

Which sizes do you stock?

Most tees and tracksuits come in XS to XXL. A few styles, such as the Halo Tee, run S to XL, and our caps are one size. The size dropdown on each product shows exactly what is available.

I ordered the wrong size. Can I exchange it?

Yes, subject to stock. Message us within 7 days of collecting your parcel. See Refund & Cancellation Policy for how exchanges work.

Ordering & Payment

How do I place an order?
  1. Choose your size and quantity, then tap Add to Cart.
  2. Open your cart and tap Checkout.
  3. Pay securely through PayFast.
  4. Send us your order confirmation on WhatsApp.
Why do I need to message you on WhatsApp after paying?

WhatsApp confirmation is how our team knows to start packing your order. Without it your order may be delayed, so please send it right after your payment goes through.

How can I pay?

All payments are processed securely by PayFast. You will see the payment options available to you when you check out. We never see or store your card or banking details.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, if it has not been dispatched yet. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible with your order details. Once it has shipped, the return process applies.

Delivery

Who delivers my order, and how does it work?

We ship with PEP PAXI, a store-to-store service. Your parcel goes to the PEP store or PAXI Point you choose, and you collect it using an SMS code sent to your cell phone number. See Shipping Details for the full process.

How long does delivery take?

We pack orders within 1–2 business days of confirmed payment. After dispatch, delivery takes 7–9 business days with Standard or 3–5 business days with Express. These are estimates, not guarantees.

How much does delivery cost?

It depends on the bag size and speed: from R59.95 (Standard Bag, Standard speed) up to R139.95 (Large Bag, Express). We do not currently offer free shipping. The full price table is on the Shipping Details page.

What if I can't collect my parcel in time?

Contact us right away. Uncollected parcels are returned to us, and we will arrange re-delivery (a new delivery fee applies) or a refund of the item price less the delivery costs already paid.

What if my parcel gets lost?

Every PAXI bag includes basic cover up to R500, and extra cover is available (a R10 voucher covers up to R2,500, a R20 voucher up to R5,000). Ask us before dispatch if you want extra cover on a higher-value order like a tracksuit. If a parcel is confirmed lost, we will resend or refund.

Returns & Refunds

Can I return something?

Yes. You have a 7-day cooling-off period from the day you collect your parcel. The item must be unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition with tags. Message us first so we can give you the return details.

What if my item is faulty or not what I ordered?

Contact us with photos as soon as you notice. We will repair, replace, or refund it, and we cover the return delivery.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 30 days of cancellation, usually sooner. Read the full Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Contact & Tracking

How do I contact you?

The quickest way is WhatsApp. You can also email discreteclothing24@gmail.com or message us on Instagram. Our website is open 24 hours, and we reply as soon as we can.

How do I check on my order?

Visit our Track Order page, or send us your order number on WhatsApp or email and we will update you.

Still stuck? Message us on WhatsApp and we will help.
