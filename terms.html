When you place an order, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address. We use this information only to:

After your payment is successful, you must send the order confirmation message to us on WhatsApp. This step is how our team knows to start preparing and packing your order — without it, your order may be delayed, since WhatsApp confirmation is currently part of how we track and action new orders.

Orders are typically packaged within 1–2 business days of confirmed payment, with delivery taking 3–5 business days within South Africa. See our Shipping & Returns page for full details.

Please double-check your sizing, address, and contact details before placing an order. If something's wrong, contact us on WhatsApp as soon as possible so we can correct it before your order ships.

Questions about these terms, your order, or your information? Reach us anytime on WhatsApp or email discreteclothing24@gmail.com.
