Sizing Guide

Every piece is cut for fit. Use this guide to find your size before ordering — we want you to feel the fabric the way it was intended.

How to Measure

01

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor. Keep it snug but not tight.

02

Length

Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the hem. Stand straight and keep the tape flat against the body.

03

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waist. For tracksuits, measure where the waistband will sit.

04

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, approximately 20cm below your natural waistline.

Between Sizes?

  • Size up for a relaxed street fit, size down for a cleaner, fitted silhouette.
  • Chest, waist and hip ranges are body measurements. Length, shoulder, sleeve and inseam are garment measurements, so lay a piece you already like flat and compare.
  • Unsure? Message us on WhatsApp or email discreteclothing24@gmail.com and we'll help you pick the right size before you order.
  • Got the wrong size anyway? We offer size exchanges, subject to stock. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
