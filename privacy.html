Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

DISCRETE respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

  • We collect only what we need to take your order and get it to you.
  • We never sell your information.
  • We only email you marketing if you signed up, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information at any time.

1. Who Is Responsible

The responsible party for your information is DISCRETE (trading as DISCRETE STUDIO). Our Information Officer is the owner of DISCRETE, reachable at discreteclothing24@gmail.com or on WhatsApp.

[ADD BEFORE LAUNCH] Registered business name and physical / street address.

2. What We Collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address, and cell phone / WhatsApp number.
  • Delivery details: the PEP store or PAXI Point you choose for collection, and any address details needed to deliver your order.
  • Order details: what you ordered (items, sizes, quantities), amounts, and the payment status and reference we receive from PayFast. We never see or store your card or banking details.
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp, Instagram, or email, such as your order confirmation.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you sign up.
  • On your device: your cart, wishlist, and cookie choice are saved in your browser so the site works the way you expect (see section 8).

3. Why We Use It

  • To process your payment, pack your order, and arrange delivery (to carry out our contract with you).
  • To contact you about your order, including WhatsApp or email updates and delivery collection details.
  • To handle returns, exchanges, refunds, and queries.
  • To send you news, drops, and offers (only with your consent).
  • To keep records the law requires and to protect the site from fraud and misuse.

4. Who We Share It With

We share information only with the people who help us serve you:

  • PayFast, which processes your payment.
  • PEP / PAXI, which delivers your parcel. They receive your name, cell number, and collection point so you can be notified and collect your order.
  • Service providers that help us run the shop, such as website hosting and order notification tools, and messaging platforms like WhatsApp.
  • Authorities, where the law requires us to.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing. Some of our providers may store or process information outside South Africa; where that happens, we only use providers that protect it to a reasonable standard.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep order and contact details for as long as we need them to fulfil your order and handle any returns or queries. Financial and order records are kept for the period South African law requires (generally five years). Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. After that we delete or anonymise the information.

6. How We Protect It

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to keep your information safe, including using PayFast's secure payment system and limiting who can see order details. No online system is perfectly secure, so please also look after your PAXI collection code and the devices you shop on.

7. Your Rights

Under POPIA you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your information (we may need to keep some order records because the law requires it).
  • Stop using your information for marketing, or withdraw consent you gave earlier.

To make a request, email discreteclothing24@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp. We may need to confirm who you are first. If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, you can also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

8. Cookies & Browser Storage

We use cookies and similar browser storage to keep your cart and wishlist, remember your cookie choice, and keep checkout working. Essential items are always on because the shop cannot work without them. Analytics and marketing items are optional and only used if you accept them in the cookie banner. You can change your choice any time with Cookie Settings in the footer of our home page, or by clearing your browser data.

9. Young People

If you are under 18, please get a parent or guardian's permission before placing an order or giving us your personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date at the top.

11. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email discreteclothing24@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.

This page is a plain-language summary of how DISCRETE handles your information. It is not a substitute for formal legal advice.
